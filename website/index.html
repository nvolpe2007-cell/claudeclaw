The Man Behind the Build

Nick Volpe — Master of His Craft

Nick Volpe founded Black Fox Construction on a simple belief: every client deserves the same level of care and craftsmanship he would demand for his own home. With over two decades of hands-on experience across residential and commercial projects, Nick brings both technical mastery and genuine passion to every job site.

Known for his direct communication, transparent pricing, and relentless attention to detail, Nick personally oversees each project from initial consultation through final walkthrough. He doesn't hand you off to a crew and disappear — he's there, invested in the outcome as much as you are.
